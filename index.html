Questions, Answered

        
What is a digital invitation or greeting page?
A shareable web page designed for an occasion, with your message, photos and little surprises, opened with a single link.

        
How do I choose and order a design?
Browse by occasion, preview any design, then tap Buy Now to start your order.

        
Can I personalise a design?
Yes. Add your names, dates, photos and message to make any design your own.

        
Are regional languages available?
Many designs come with regional-language options. You can switch the language in the preview.

        
Can I get something fully custom?
Yes. Use the Custom Websites form to tell us your idea.

        
Will it work on phones?
Yes. Every design is made to look lovely on phones, tablets and computers.
